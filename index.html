Instructor page. Students only ever see the pages linked under "Students".

Session 1: Mini brief vs outcome

Instructor: open the board on the projector · print the six team half sheets (odd teams Brief 1, even teams Brief 2)

Students: their sheet's QR opens s1/team.html?b=1 or ?b=2; voting is s1/vote.html (QR on the board, key Q)

Board keys: 1 concepts · 2 vote 1 · 3 vote 2 · 4 reveal · V unveil all · N cover the brand name · M add a concept by hand · 5 images · G generate an image per concept (Seedream 5.0 Pro on Kie, about 7 credits and a minute each; Shift+G regenerates all) · X export JSON

G asks for your Kie API key once per browser tab and keeps it only in that tab (sessionStorage). It is never stored in the site or sent anywhere but Kie. Test it once tonight with one concept.

Session 2: Creative quality evaluation

Instructor: open the board on the projector

Students: one page for all four ads, s2/score.html (QR on the board, key Q)

Board keys: 1 to 4 pick the ad · H hide or show results · E reveal the expected score one letter at a time (Shift+E all) · B scoreboard · W gut words · X export JSON

Before class

  1. Open both boards on the room's Wi-Fi. The badge top right should say live. If it says offline, the relay is unreachable: Session 1 falls back to key M (type the concepts in), Session 2 to paper cards and a hand count.
  2. Scan one QR with your phone and send a test. It should appear on the board within a second or two.
  3. To start a clean room, change LIVE_KEY in config.js, or add ?k=anything to every page you open and print the sheets from that address. Test messages expire from the relay after 12 hours anyway.
